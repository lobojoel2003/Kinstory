# Printing your book

Kinstory produces two PDFs:

- **`interior.pdf`**: every page of the book, in reading order.
- **`cover.pdf`**: one wide page with the back cover, spine and front cover,
  plus bleed on every edge.

## Choosing settings

| Setting               | Guidance                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trim size             | 6 × 9 in suits most memoirs. Choose 7 × 10 or 8 × 10 for many photos. Use A5 if your printer works in metric sizes.                     |
| Where will you print? | _Lulu_: bleed on all four edges. _Amazon KDP / IngramSpark_: bleed on the outside, top and bottom. _Home printing or screen_: no bleed. |
| Ink                   | Black and white is much cheaper per copy; photos are converted to greyscale for you.                                                    |
| Paper                 | Cream reads warmly and suits memoirs; white suits photo-heavy books. This also sets the spine width.                                    |

Choose these **before** typesetting: page size, bleed and spine width are baked
into the PDFs.

## Ordering from Lulu

1. Create a print book: _Paperback, Perfect Bound_, the same trim size and
   paper colour.
2. Upload `interior.pdf` as the interior.
3. For the cover, choose _Upload your cover_ and upload `cover.pdf`. Lulu
   checks that the spine matches the page count.
4. Order a single proof copy before ordering for the family.

## Ordering from Amazon KDP or IngramSpark

1. Set the trim size and choose **Bleed** for the interior.
2. Upload `interior.pdf`.
3. Upload `cover.pdf` as a print-ready cover. KDP prints its barcode on the
   lower right of the back cover; Kinstory leaves that area empty.
4. Use the private or proof-copy option if the book is only for family.

## At a local print shop

Choose _Home printing or screen_ and ask for the interior printed double-sided
and bound (perfect binding or a hardcover case). Take `cover.pdf` along, or
ask the shop to design a case cover from it.

## Checklist before you print

- Read the whole PDF once on a large screen, and once aloud for the stories
  that matter most.
- Check photo captions and the names of people in them.
- Most printers need at least **24 pages** (KDP) or **32 pages** (Lulu) for a
  paperback. Blank pages count; add stories or photos if you are close.
- Typeset again after any change. The PDFs are not updated on their own.
